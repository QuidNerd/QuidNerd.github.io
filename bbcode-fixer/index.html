Fonts

Apple devices (macOS/iOS) have their own font system, because of course they do, but as of a December 2025 July 2026 update the regular font has been unified to Georgia Bitstream Charter across the board. RIP Iowan Old Style — genuinely thrilled to see it go, one less thing to account for when coding across systems. and RIP Georgia too, that lasted all of seven months.

anyway that's settled now. ANOTHER update bc why not: they've stopped choosing for us entirely — you can now pick the regular font yourself, between Bitstream Charter (the new one), Georgia (the old one), and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next — an san serif accessibility font built to be as legible as possible, especially for anyone with vision or reading difficulties. so instead of one thing zero things to account for, it is now three. cool. cool cool cool.
